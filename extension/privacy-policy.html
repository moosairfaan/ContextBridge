ContextBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Summary ContextBridge stores everything on your device. We do not collect personal information, run analytics, or send your data to any external server.

This privacy policy describes how the ContextBridge Chrome extension (“ContextBridge,” “the extension,” “we”) handles information when you install and use it. ContextBridge helps you capture and reuse conversation context across supported AI chat websites in your browser.

Who we are

ContextBridge is a browser extension published for Google Chrome. This policy applies only to the extension and its bundled pages (such as the popup). It does not govern the AI platforms you visit; those sites have their own privacy policies.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. ContextBridge does not ask for your name, email address, account credentials, or any other information that identifies you.

The extension may read text visible on supported AI chat pages so it can build compressed context and optionally insert that context into a composer field. That processing happens in your browser. We do not receive a copy of that text on our servers because we do not operate servers for this extension.

Where your data is stored

All data ContextBridge saves stays on your device, inside your browser profile, using the Chrome extension storage API (chrome.storage.local) only.

Stored data may include, for example:

  • Compressed conversation context and session metadata
  • Extension settings (such as injection and compression preferences)

This data is not synced to ContextBridge servers. It is subject to Chrome’s normal extension storage limits and remains until you remove it or uninstall the extension.

No external transmission

No data is ever sent to external servers by ContextBridge for storage, analytics, advertising, or any other purpose. The extension does not include third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or remote logging.

Network activity you see while using AI platforms (for example, when ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity communicate with their own services) is between you and those websites, not ContextBridge.

No analytics or tracking

ContextBridge does not use analytics, session recording, fingerprinting, or behavioral tracking. We do not monitor which prompts you send or how often you use each platform beyond what is needed locally to provide extension features.

Which sites the extension can access

ContextBridge only runs on AI platform pages declared in the extension manifest. Host access and content scripts are limited to:

  • https://chat.openai.com/*
  • https://www.chat.openai.com/*
  • https://chatgpt.com/*
  • https://www.chatgpt.com/*
  • https://claude.ai/*
  • https://www.claude.ai/*
  • https://gemini.google.com/*
  • https://www.gemini.google.com/*
  • https://perplexity.ai/*
  • https://www.perplexity.ai/*

The extension does not request broad access to all websites. It uses permissions such as storage, tabs, activeTab, and scripting only as needed to operate on those supported origins.

How you can delete your data

You can clear all data ContextBridge has saved at any time. Options include:

  • Using controls inside the extension popup (for example, clearing a session or editing stored context)
  • Running chrome.storage.local.clear() from the extension’s service worker or developer tools, which removes every key stored by this extension in local storage
  • Uninstalling ContextBridge from Chrome, which removes extension data

After clearing storage or uninstalling, previously saved sessions and settings cannot be recovered from ContextBridge.

Children’s privacy

ContextBridge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy if the extension’s behavior or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or ContextBridge data practices, contact the developer through the support channel listed on the Chrome Web Store listing for ContextBridge.
